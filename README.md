# AboKhloud’s Photo Fetcher

Website and privacy policy for **AboKhloud’s Photo Fetcher**, a browser extension that collects every photo of a film from its IMDb page at full resolution, removes logos and icons, and sorts the rest into posters, stills, events and behind-the-scenes.

- `index.html`: the website (Arabic and English)
- `privacy.html`: the extension's privacy policy
